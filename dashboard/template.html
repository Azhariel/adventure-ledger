Start a Neutral price book

Auctionator keeps one price book for the realm, so a scan at the goblin auction house used to land in your faction's prices. Ledger Link now catches those scans and keeps them apart.

  1. Ledger Link 0.2 is in your AddOns folder. Log in (or /reload) so it loads.
  2. Open the auction house in Gadgetzan, Booty Bay or Everlook and scan as usual. Ledger Link says in chat that it's keeping those prices apart.
  3. /reload or log out, then rebuild. This page fills in.

/ledger neutral both also lets the scans reach Auctionator; /ledger neutral off stops keeping them apart. Faction prices from before this update may still include a Neutral scan on days you visited one.

      What this is

      Adventure Ledger turns what your WoW Forever addons already save into one page: how you level, where your gold comes from and goes, what you fight, what you own across every character, and what the auction house is doing, with forecasts where there's enough history. Nothing here comes from a server: every number is read from the SavedVariables files on your own computer.

      1. Play with the addons on. WoW writes their files when you /reload or log out, never in between.
      2. Run wowstats to rebuild this page, or wowstats live for a page that rebuilds itself every time WoW saves, with a Live view for a second monitor.
      3. Pick a character above the menu and open any module. The address bar remembers where you were.

      ~/character

      The character picked above the menu.

      Overview
      XP over played time with a forecast to your next level, hours spent in each level with a curve carried to 60, and a diary of your play sessions.
      Progress
      Where your XP comes from (quests, kills, dungeons, exploring), profession skill over time, what you made and gathered, how you spend your time, time per zone, and your flights: routes, fares and time in the air.
      Gold
      Gold on hand over played time with its trend, where gold comes from and goes, net worth (gold plus what your items would sell for), and your last farming session from Haul.
      Combat & quests
      The mobs you fight most, every quest turned in, deaths and close calls, and from the game's damage meter: damage per second, damage by spell, what hurts you, and damage at each level.
      Replay
      Plays back a session on the zone map: your path draws itself, and level-ups, deaths, quests, flights and loot appear where they happened. Scrub, zoom and pan the map, and click a moment to jump there.

      ~/account

      Every character together.

      Characters
      Your alts side by side: level, class, played time, gold, items and net worth, last played.
      Inventory
      Everything you own in bags, bank and mail, with who holds it and what it's worth.
      Mail to alts
      Items one character holds that another crafts with or needs to restock.
      Drop rates
      What each kind of mob drops for you, how often, and gold per hour at your pace (DropLab).

      ~/live

      Live
      Only with wowstats live. Follows the combat log while you play: XP to level, your position on the zone map, fights, kills, players around you and your newest screenshot. WoW writes the log every 5 minutes, so it runs a little behind.

      ~/market

      From your Auctionator scans: each scan day keeps the lowest and highest price and how many were listed. The Faction / Neutral switch at the top picks which auction house the first four modules use.

      Signals
      Your stockpile's value per scan day with a forecast, your best sellers, and deals: items far under their usual price, and items you own far over it.
      Stockpile
      What you hold, against its launch low, peak and current supply, with a signal when it's a good time to sell.
      Browse
      The whole price database, or one family of trade goods, with search.
      Movers
      What's rising fastest and what has fallen furthest.
      Faction vs Neutral
      Items priced at both houses, the gap between them, and what a flip makes after each house's cut.
      Your trades
      Your auction house ledger: buys, sales, deposits and buyers, in total and per item.
      Crafting
      Your recipes ranked by profit at today's prices, with each one's full reagent tree, and your restock list (Make or Buy).

      Click any item row to open the item inspector: both houses' prices on one chart, the forecast, and how many were listed.

      Reading the page

      • Colours have fixed roles: cyan is your faction's auction house, orange the Neutral one, and pink dashes and bands are forecasts.
      • The number beside a menu entry counts what's in it.
      • Click a column header to sort; the search boxes filter as you type.
      • Item, creature and quest names open on Wowhead; player names open on the WoW Forever armory.
      • A module with nothing to show says which addon it's waiting for.
      • Addons describes each addon the page reads and says when one needs an update; its number in the menu counts the ones that need you.

      Where the data comes from

      Included
      Logbook (your journey), Ledger Link (live combat log, Neutral prices), Make or Buy (recipes), Stockpile (what you hold), Haul (farming sessions), DropLab (drop rates).
      Others it reads
      Auctionator (prices), and Questie's item names.
      Privacy
      Your data stays on your computer: the page only reads your saved files and sends nothing anywhere. It loads Chart.js and two fonts (Google Fonts) from the internet, asks GitHub for the newest version of each of our addons (at most every 6 hours), and zone map art from Wowhead: the live server downloads each zone once, and the Replay loads a zone it hasn't cached straight from Wowhead's image server.

      Updates

      1. Get the new version: git pull in the Adventure Ledger folder, or download it again from GitHub.
      2. Run install.bat. It replaces the old copies in WoW (a -Dev setup is already linked).
      3. Restart WoW: it reads an addon's version only at startup. Then run wowstats again.

      The other addons come from CurseForge: update them with the CurseForge app or from each one's page.
